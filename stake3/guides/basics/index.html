Concept

What proof-of-stake means in practice.

Proof-of-stake networks rely on participants who lock or delegate tokens so validators can help confirm transactions and maintain consensus. Instead of using proof-of-work mining equipment, these systems align security incentives around staked capital and validator performance.

Risk and responsibility

Why beginners should look beyond the word “yield”.

Staking can look simple when presented only as passive income, yet new users still face lock-up periods, validator-quality decisions, potential slashing, and exposure to token price volatility. The educational value comes from seeing all of those variables at the same time.

04

Lock-up periods

Some networks require tokens to remain staked for a set period or impose delays before funds become liquid again.
