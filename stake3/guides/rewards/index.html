Understanding rewards

Why the displayed rate never tells the whole story.

Staking rewards are usually quoted as annualized figures, but real user outcomes depend on validator fees, compounding behavior, claim schedules, tax treatment, and token price changes. A healthy staking education site needs to spell out those layers explicitly.

Liquidity and compounding

The practical trade-offs behind a reward strategy.

A strategy that maximizes theoretical yield may not fit a user who values optionality or short-term access to capital. Unbonding delays, liquid staking choices, and manual versus automatic compounding should all be considered together.
