FAQ sector

Questions readers usually ask before staking.

The accordion below is powered by the shared JavaScript added for this task, so every page now includes a working FAQ interaction in addition to complete internal navigation and smooth section scrolling.

Crypto staking is the process of locking or delegating supported tokens to help a proof-of-stake network validate transactions and stay secure. In exchange, token holders may earn rewards, but the outcome depends on protocol rules, validator quality, participation rates, fees, and market conditions.
Many beginners see staking marketed as passive income only. Ethersfi reframes the subject with context about risk, lockups, token volatility, operational reliability, and the difference between projected yields and realized results.
Review token utility, validator history, lock-up rules, unbonding periods, reward frequency, fees, tax treatment in your jurisdiction, and whether you need immediate liquidity. The guides and tools pages are designed to walk through these checkpoints step by step.
