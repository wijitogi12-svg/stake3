Core scenarios

Three ways staking conditions can evolve from here.

Forecasting in staking should remain conditional. Ethersfi uses narrative scenarios to help users understand what may happen if adoption accelerates, if participation saturates, or if regulation changes product availability.

01

Participation boom

If more token holders stake over time, headline yields may decline while network security and validator competition improve. Users benefit from context, not just the lower APR number.

Signals to watch

The inputs that shape any reasonable staking outlook.

Good predictions depend on actual network structure. That means watching token emissions, validator counts, restaking narratives, fee structures, and whether users are choosing custodial or direct delegation paths.
